# Unity

[Home](README.md)

## What it is

Unity is a persuasion principle based on the idea of shared identity. People are often more likely to respond positively to a message when they feel that the person, brand, or group communicating with them is part of the same group or shares something important with them.

Unity goes beyond simply liking someone. It focuses on the feeling of “we” and a sense of belonging. This connection can come from shared interests, experiences, values, communities, goals, or identities.

## Recognize and apply it

Unity can be recognized when a message emphasizes belonging, togetherness, shared experiences, or a common identity. Words such as “we,” “us,” “our,” “together,” and “community” can help create this feeling.

In design and communication, Unity can be applied by:

- Showing people who share a common goal or experience.
- Using language that makes the audience feel included.
- Emphasizing community and belonging.
- Connecting the product or message to values shared by the audience.
- Using visuals and messages that reinforce the idea that the audience is part of something larger.

## Example 1: Apple

Apple often presents its products as tools for people who create, communicate, work, and express themselves. Its marketing can create a sense of connection among people who identify with creativity, technology, and innovation.

**How Unity is used:** The audience can see themselves as part of a larger community of people who use technology to create and communicate.

**Source:** [Apple](https://www.apple.com/)

## Example 2: Nike

Nike frequently uses messages connected to athletes, movement, determination, and shared experiences in sports. These messages can create a sense that different people belong to a larger community connected through physical activity and personal goals.

**How Unity is used:** The message connects individuals through a shared identity around sports, effort, and achievement.

**Source:** [Nike](https://www.nike.com/)

## How I would use Unity

I would use Unity by making the audience feel included in the message rather than presenting the brand as separate from them. I would use inclusive language, shared goals, and visuals showing people connected by similar interests or experiences.

For the Caregiver, Ruler, and Creator archetypes, Unity can support each archetype in a different way. For the Caregiver, it can emphasize community, support, and caring for one another. For the Ruler, it can emphasize belonging to a confident and organized group. For the Creator, it can connect people through shared creativity, imagination, and self-expression.
