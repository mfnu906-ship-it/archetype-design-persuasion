## What it is

Authority is a persuasion principle based on the idea that people are more likely to trust or follow a message when it comes from someone they view as knowledgeable, credible, or experienced.

Authority can be established through expertise, professional credentials, experience, trusted organizations, or recognition from others. When people are uncertain about a decision, evidence of expertise can make a message or recommendation feel more reliable.

## Recognize and apply it

Authority can be recognized when a message emphasizes expertise, credibility, experience, professional knowledge, or recognition from trusted sources.

In design and communication, Authority can be applied by:

- Highlighting expert knowledge or experience.
- Displaying professional credentials or qualifications.
- Including endorsements from credible experts or organizations.
- Using statistics or research from reliable sources.
- Showing awards, certifications, or other signs of expertise.

## Example 1: Colgate

Colgate uses professional dental expertise in its marketing and educational content. The brand provides information about oral health and frequently connects its products and recommendations with dental professionals and expert knowledge.

**How Authority is used:** Professional dental knowledge gives the audience a reason to view the brand's oral-care information and products as credible.

**Source:** [Colgate](https://www.colgate.com/)

## Example 2: Mayo Clinic

Mayo Clinic provides health information supported by medical professionals and research. Its expertise and reputation as a medical organization give its information credibility when people are looking for health guidance.

**How Authority is used:** The audience is more likely to trust the information because it comes from an established medical organization with professional expertise.

**Source:** [Mayo Clinic](https://www.mayoclinic.org/)

## How I would use Authority

I would use Authority by giving the audience a clear reason to trust the message. I would emphasize expertise, credible sources, professional experience, or other evidence showing that the information or product is reliable.

For the Hero and Magician archetypes, Authority can support each archetype in a different way. For the Hero, expert guidance can make a product focused on achievement and improvement feel more credible. For the Magician, Authority can show that the tools or knowledge behind a transformation are supported by real expertise.
